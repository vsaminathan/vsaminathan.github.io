Core capabilities

More insight into every webpage.

Explore important webpage quality signals without switching between multiple tools.

SEO metadata analysis

Review page titles, descriptions, canonical information, social tags, and heading structure.

Accessibility analysis

Identify potential accessibility issues using axe-core, covering WCAG 2.0, 2.1, and 2.2 Level A and AA criteria.

Image diagnostics

Find broken or failed image loads and identify images that may be missing alternative text.

Resource diagnostics

Examine failed page and resource requests, including CSS, JavaScript, fonts, and media.

Actionable findings

Understand identified issues through explanations and suggested fixes.

Element inspection

Highlight affected elements and copy CSS selectors for further analysis.
