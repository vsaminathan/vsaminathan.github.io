# PageQualis Privacy Policy
<div class="last-updated">Last updated: November 11, 2026</div>

PageQualis is a browser extension that checks the web page you are viewing for common content-quality
and accessibility issues.

**What PageQualis does with page content:** When you scan a page, PageQualis reads the page's structure
(for example its title, headings, images and links) inside your browser and shows the results in the side
panel. It also runs a bundled copy of the open-source accessibility engine axe-core over the page, in your
browser. Page content and scan results are processed entirely within your browser. PageQualis does not upload
or transmit this information to any external server.

**What PageQualis keeps:** Scan results are temporarily retained in the extension's memory so you can switch between up to 10 scanned tabs and return to their results without running another scan. This information is not saved to disk or synchronized across devices. The results are discarded when the side panel closes, the page reloads, or the tab is closed.

**What PageQualis does not do:** PageQualis does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal information, browsing history, page content, cookies or credentials. Page content is accessed only temporarily, within your browser, to perform the requested scan. It does not send data to any server, analytics
service or third party. It makes no network requests of its own. All processing happens locally on your device.

**Clipboard access:** If you click the copy button next to a finding, PageQualis places that element's selector (or a
short console snippet that finds it) on your clipboard. It never reads your clipboard and never copies anything
without a click.

**The one change it makes to a page:** When you click a finding, PageQualis draws a temporary outline around
the affected element so you can see it. The outline is automatically removed after a few seconds, when you click elsewhere on the page, or when you run another scan.

**Permissions:** 
- activeTab: Allows PageQualis to access the currently active tab after you click its toolbar icon.
- scripting: Allows PageQualis to run its content-quality and accessibility checks on the active tab.
- sidePanel: Allows PageQualis to display scan results in the browser's side panel. 

**External Links:** A finding from the accessibility engine can include a "Why this matters" link to the engine publisher's
explanation of the rule. It opens in a new tab only when you click it, and is marked with an arrow icon. Nothing about
your page is added to the link, and it is opened without sending a referrer.

**Third-party code:** PageQualis includes axe-core, an open-source accessibility testing engine maintained by Deque Systems and distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. It is bundled with the extension and runs locally in your browser. It is not downloaded at runtime and does not independently transmit page content or scan results.

**Chrome Web Store User Data Policy:** PageQualis's use of information accessed through Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

**Changes to this policy:** Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page, along with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes to how PageQualis handles information will be clearly communicated to users.
