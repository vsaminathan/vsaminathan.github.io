# PageQualis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/06/2026

PageQualis is a browser extension that checks the web page you are viewing for common content-quality
and accessibility issues.

**What PageQualis does with page content:** When you scan a page, PageQualis reads the page's structure
(for example its title, headings, images and links) inside your browser and shows the results in the side
panel. It also runs a bundled copy of the open-source accessibility engine axe-core over the page, in your
browser. Nothing is uploaded.

**What PageQualis keeps:** The results of a scan are held in the side panel's memory while it is open, so that
you can return to a tab and see them again (up to 10 tabs). They are not written to disk, not synced, and they are
discarded when the panel closes, the page reloads, or the tab is closed.

**What PageQualis does not do:** PageQualis does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data,
browsing history, page content, cookies or credentials. It does not send data to any server, analytics
service or third party. It makes no network requests of its own. All processing happens locally on your device.

**The clipboard:** If you click the copy button next to a finding, PageQualis places that element's selector (or a
short console snippet that finds it) on your clipboard. It never reads your clipboard and never copies anything
without a click.

**The one change it makes to a page:** When you click a finding, PageQualis draws a temporary outline around
the affected element so you can see it. The outline is removed again after a few seconds, when you click the page,
or when you rescan, and the page's own content is never edited, saved or published.

**Permissions:** PageQualis uses the `activeTab` permission to access only the tab you are using, only after
you click its toolbar icon; `scripting` to run its bundled scanner and accessibility engine on that tab; and
`sidePanel` to display results.

**Links:** A finding from the accessibility engine can include a "Why this matters" link to the engine publisher's
explanation of the rule. It opens in a new tab only when you click it, and is marked with an arrow icon. Nothing about
your page is added to the link, and it is opened without sending a referrer.

**Third-party code:** PageQualis includes axe-core, an open-source accessibility engine, licensed under the Mozilla Public
License 2.0. It is packaged inside the extension and runs locally; it is not downloaded at runtime and it
sends nothing anywhere.
